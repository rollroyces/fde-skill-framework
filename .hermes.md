# .hermes.md — FDE Skill Framework Configuration

This file is loaded into every Hermes session in this project. It is the
contract between FDE work and the business. If a row below is wrong, fix it
here — don't work around it.

---

## Business Metrics (what we are paid to move)

| Metric ID | Name                                  | Definition                                                                 | Direction | Default SLA target       |
|-----------|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------|--------------------------|
| M-ROI     | Customer ROI positive                 | Engagements ending in `decision: scale` with delta-M ≥ 80% of target        | up        | ≥ 70% of engagements     |
| M-CYCLE   | Discovery → vertical-slice time       | Calendar days from sponsor kickoff to vertical slice in customer env        | down      | ≤ 14 business days       |
| M-SLA     | SLA breach rate                       | Engagements shipping in the quarter that breach a contracted SLA at GA     | down      | ≤ 10%                    |
| M-REWORK  | Post-GA critical defects per engagement| P0/P1 incidents in first 30 days after GA, per engagement                   | down      | ≤ 1                      |
| M-COST    | Engineering cost vs ROI-model forecast| (Actual eng hours × loaded rate + infra) / predicted total cost            | near 1.0  | 0.8 ≤ x ≤ 1.2            |

These are the *only* metrics the FDE team is measured on at the program
level. Engagement-level metrics are picked during Discovery and live in
`engagements/<customer>-<date>.md`.

## SLA Targets (contract defaults — override per engagement)

These are the ceilings a sponsor is told about before work starts. Tighter
SLA → longer build time and higher cost; relax where the customer will allow.

| Surface        | Latency p50 | Latency p95 | Latency p99 | Availability | Error budget (30d) |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Customer API   | 80 ms       | 250 ms      | 500 ms      | 99.9%        | 0.1%              |
| Internal batch | 5 min       | 20 min      | 60 min      | 99.5%        | 0.5%              |
| Human-in-loop  | 4 business hrs ack | 8 business hrs first response | 1 business day resolution | 99.0% | n/a |

Per-engagement SLAs are negotiated in Discovery and recorded in the
engagement log. Never tighten an SLA mid-engagement without a change order.

## Tech Stack Constraints

Hard constraints. If a customer request violates one, surface the conflict
in Discovery — do not silently work around it.

- **Languages.** Production code in Python 3.11+ or TypeScript 5+. Bash
  allowed only for build/test glue (<200 lines, no business logic).
- **Frameworks.** Web: FastAPI or Express. Frontend: React or server-rendered
  HTML. No new framework introductions inside an engagement without sponsor
  sign-off.
- **Data.** Customer data never leaves the customer's cloud account or
  the agreed-upon region. No cross-region replication without a written
  addendum. PII must be tagged at ingest; downstream services refuse
  untagged data.
- **Secrets.** All secrets via the customer's KMS / Vault. No long-lived
  static credentials. Service accounts scoped to one service, rotated ≤90d.
- **Observability.** OpenTelemetry traces + Prometheus metrics as the
  baseline. Logs in the customer's SIEM-compatible store. Every customer-
  facing endpoint emits: request id, customer id, latency, result, error
  class.
- **Vendors.** Third-party APIs require: documented SLA from the vendor,
  data-processing agreement on file, and a tested circuit breaker in our
  integration. No "we'll add the breaker later".
- **Deployment.** IaC checked into the engagement repo. No `terraform apply`
  from a laptop — CI pipeline only. Production deploys require one reviewer
  outside the FDE pair.
- **Compliance.** GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2 controls are baseline. PIPL (China),
  FedRAMP (US public sector), and ITAR require a separate compliance review
  before Discovery completes.

## Stakeholder Communication

- **Daily.** One paragraph to the sponsor and named daily contact: blockers
  first, decisions needed, then progress. Sent before 09:00 in the
  sponsor's local time, every business day the engagement is active.
- **Weekly.** Live demo of working software against real customer data.
  No slides unless explicitly requested.
- **Milestone.** Solution brief sent before Architecture exits; ROI
  evaluation appended to the engagement log after every meaningful ship.
- **Incident.** Customer-impacting incident → page within 5 minutes, written
  RCA within 5 business days, regardless of customer request.

## ROI Tracking Discipline

Every engagement keeps an ROI model in
`engagements/<customer>-<date>.md`. The model has three sections, all
sourced:

1. **Value side.** Revenue enabled OR hours saved × loaded cost. Numbers
   come from the sponsor, not the FDE's gut.
2. **Cost side.** Engineering hours × loaded rate + infra + third-party
   API costs. Predicted at Architecture exit; actuals tracked weekly.
3. **Decision rule.** Before build starts, write the sentence: "If at GA
   the value side is less than 1.2× the cost side, we cut." That sentence
   is binding — the engagement lead signs it.
